## Parcel data

Real parcel geometry is fetched per county by `scripts/fetch-parcels.ts` from the
relevant county's public GIS portal. Those datasets are published by Georgia
county governments as public records; check the individual county's terms before
redistributing their geometry.

**No owner names are read, stored, or redistributed.** Owner-identifying fields
are dropped at ingest, and the names shown in game are generated from the parcel
id. See the privacy note in `src/engine/parcels/types.ts`.

Where a county's real cadastre has not been fetched, the repository ships a
procedurally generated stand-in produced by `scripts/build-synthetic-parcels.ts`,
flagged `synthetic: true` and labelled as such in the game.
